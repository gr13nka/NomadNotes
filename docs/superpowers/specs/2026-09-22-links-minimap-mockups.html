Map
  • Radial layout, recomputed per root: current page in the centre, its neighbours spaced evenly around it.
  • No motion: recentring redraws the layout instantly, it never animates.
  • Links are shown without direction, one hop from the current page — no depth control.
  • Tap a chip to select it; [Open] or [Centre] then act on the selection.
  • [⤢] expands the map to fill the page, for a larger view.
